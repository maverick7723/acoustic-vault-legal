\# Privacy Policy for Acoustic Vault Dashcam



\*\*Effective Date:\*\* August 20, 2026  

\*\*Status:\*\* Zero-Trust / Offline-First



Acoustic Vault Dashcam is built from the ground up with a "Privacy by Architecture" philosophy. This app is designed to be a strictly offline evidence-capture tool.



\## 1. Zero Data Collection

We do not collect, transmit, or share any user data. 

\- \*\*Audio Recordings:\*\* All audio captured via the microphone is encrypted in real-time on your device. We never have access to your audio files.

\- \*\*Personal Identifiers:\*\* We do not collect names, email addresses, or phone numbers.

\- \*\*Telemetry \& Analytics:\*\* There are no third-party tracking SDKs, crash reporters, or analytics tools integrated into this app.



\## 2. Local Encryption \& Security

The app utilizes industry-standard AES-256-GCM encryption to secure your recordings. 

\- \*\*Master Key:\*\* Your encryption keys are stored in your device's hardware-backed Keystore or derived locally from your Master PIN.

\- \*\*Google Identity:\*\* Google Sign-In is used strictly to retrieve a unique Subject ID \*locally\* on your device. This ID acts as a cryptographic salt for your Master Key. This identifier is never transmitted to our servers or any third party.



\## 3. Data Sovereignty \& Storage

Your data stays on your device.

\- \*\*Local Storage:\*\* Encrypted files are stored in your app's private sandbox or your chosen local folder via the Storage Access Framework (SAF).

\- \*\*No Cloud Backups:\*\* We do not provide cloud storage. If you delete the app or lose your device, your recordings cannot be recovered by us.



\## 4. User Control \& Deletion

You have total control over your data.

\- \*\*Manual Deletion:\*\* You can delete individual recordings at any time.

\- \*\*Nuclear Wipe:\*\* The app includes a "Panic Button" feature that permanently destroys all local encryption keys, rendering all existing recordings mathematically impossible to recover.



\## 5. Internet Usage

The app requires the INTERNET permission solely for the initial local authentication with Google Identity services to derive your unique key. No data is transmitted during or after this process.

Since we do not collect any data, we have no way to identify you. For technical support, please contact the developer via E-Mail: gc116815@gmail.com.
